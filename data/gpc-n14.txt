Checks
------

- all 4,096 residues were present and disjoint;
- every shard had one content-validated completion marker;
- every output contained exactly one final summary;
- every shard recorded the same binary hash;
- the total agreed with the published unlabeled-poset count A000112(14);
- exactly one class was a chain;
- certificate counters partitioned the total;
- the open counter was zero.

The published total is an external check on enumeration completeness.
The shard conditions and partition identity are self-consistency checks.
The zero open count is the computational result.
