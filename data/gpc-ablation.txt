Controlled GPC bilateral-screen timing ablation
===============================================

Date: 26 July 2026

The baseline was the exact source and binary used for the order-14
calculation. The comparison build removed only the successful early return
from the bilateral low-slave screen; all subsequent exact recurrences were
unchanged.

Both programs were compiled with GCC 13.3.0 and run on the production
platform (AMD EPYC 7B13). One process was pinned to a single CPU, the builds
were warmed up, and their order was alternated between repetitions. Five
repetitions were made through order 12 and three at order 13. Order 10 is a
complete pass; the other rows sum the same three fixed modulus residues. The
table reports median user times.

order  workload                         baseline (s)  no screen (s)  ratio  range
-----  -------------------------------  ------------  -------------  -----  ------------
   10  complete                                 1.84           7.80  4.245  4.232--4.286
   11  K=16, residues 0,5,11                    8.54          41.15  4.813  4.797--4.826
   12  K=256, residues 0,85,170                17.50          97.58  5.571  5.563--5.586
   13  K=4096, residues 0,1365,2730            56.93         486.70  8.551  8.526--8.570

For every paired invocation, the total, chain count, invariant
pair-certificate union, balanced-triple count, and open count agreed exactly.
This measures the bilateral screen within the present implementation. It is
not a controlled comparison with the programs of Peczarski or De Loof, De
Meyer, and De Baets.
