Linear-extension-majority cycle regression
==========================================

The companion full-pair program reproduces the following published values.

order  cyclic classes  length 3  length 4
-----  --------------  --------  --------
    9               5         5         0
   10             153       148         6
   11           5,815     5,740       101

Source: K. De Loof, B. De Baets, and H. De Meyer, "Counting linear
extension majority cycles in partially ordered sets on up to 13 elements,"
Computers & Mathematics with Applications 59 (2010), 1541--1547, Table 2,
doi:10.1016/j.camwa.2009.12.021.

The order-10 value was recomputed with:

  build/majority_census 10 o q

Order 11 was divided into four deterministic residues,

  build/majority_census 11 o q m <residue> 4

and the four summaries were summed. The aggregate certificate partition
closed and the total was 46,749,427, the published number of unlabeled
order-11 posets.

The program shares the ideal-lattice enumeration and extension-counting
routines used by the GPC verifier. Its source SHA-256 is:
